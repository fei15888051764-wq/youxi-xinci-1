You're 12 minutes into a ranked match when someone types this word in all-chat. They're not talking about a bar. They're talking about the beautiful, petty art of making someone miss.

In gaming, this is a fake-out. A zig when they expect a zag. It's the [MOBA](/slang/moba-gaming/) player darting left then flashing right to dodge a hook. The FPS player faking behind cover to make the sniper whiff. It's not just dodging—it's dodging with style.

## How It Became a Thing

The word comes from American football, where it's a sharp fake to shake a defender. The root is Gullah "joog," meaning disorderly or wicked. Fitting, because a good fake-out feels downright evil.

It entered gaming through sports titles and fighting games, then exploded in MOBAs. Dota 2 and League of Legends turned this into a science. Faking into fog of war. Faking around terrain. Faking with ability animations. The word became shorthand for "I outplayed you without even fighting."

## Why Gamers Can't Quit It

This move is the purest form of skill expression. It requires zero resources—just movement and game sense. A perfectly timed fake is better than a [flash](/slang/flash-gaming/) because it costs nothing and humiliates everything.

The difference between this and a dodge is intention. A dodge is reactive. You see the ability, you get out. This is predictive. You move *before* they commit, baiting them to whiff. It's mind games at the most basic level, and it's satisfying every single time.

## When to Fake vs When to Just Run

Here's a rule I learned the hard way: if you have more than 40% HP and the enemy has a skillshot, go for it. If you're under 20% HP and they have point-and-click, just run. Styling on someone is fun. Dying while styling is embarrassing. Ngl, I've fed more kills trying to style than I'd like to admit.

## FAQ

**Is this the same as dodge?**

Kinda, but this has more sauce. Dodge is "I got out of the way." This is "I made you think I was going left." The deception is the point.

**How do I actually do this in League?**

Use fog of war. Use minions. Use bushes. Change direction right after you enter unseen terrain. If they can't see you, they can't predict you. That's the whole trick.

**How do I explain this to a non-gamer?**

Fake left, go right. It's the oldest move in sports, and it works in games because human brains love patterns. Break the pattern, win the fight.

**When should I NOT do this?**

When you're already dead anyway. If you're 100 HP and the enemy has a point-and-click ult, just run. Don't style on them—just survive.
