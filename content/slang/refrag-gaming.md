The first time I heard this term, I thought it was an insult. Turns out—well, sometimes it is. If your [IGL](/slang/igl-gaming/) is screaming "get the refrag" and you're already dead, it's definitely an insult.

When it works, though, this move is poetry. Your entry fragger dies peeking site. You swing out and drop his killer instantly. The round stays alive. That's the play. It's not just a [trade](/slang/trade-gaming/)—it's the specific moment of payback that prevents a collapse.

## Why It Matters

In tactical FPS, numbers are everything. A 5v5 is chess. A 4v5 is chaos. This kill is the bridge between them. It buys time, resets the defense, and keeps the attackers from flooding through the gap your teammate left.

Pro teams drill this constantly. In CS2 and Valorant, the second player in a stack is literally called the "refrag." His job isn't to get the first kill—it's to get the second one. The one that matters.

## The Refrag Mindset

I've noticed something weird in ranked: the players who get the best payback kills aren't the ones with the best aim. They're the ones who expect their teammate to die. Sounds dark, but it's true. If you're already holding the angle before your entry swings, the follow-up is free. If you're watching TikTok on your second monitor, your teammate dies for nothing.

## In the Wild

**You've definitely heard this one:**

> **IGL:** "Entry's dead. Get the refrag, get the refrag!"
> **You:** *swings and trades the killer*
> **IGL:** "Nice. Now we have site."

Simple. Brutal. Essential. This is the most team-oriented kill in FPS. It's not about your KDA. It's about keeping the round winnable.

## Before It Was Everywhere

The term comes from Counter-Strike 1.6, though the concept existed earlier. "Re-frag" literally meant getting the frag back after losing one. Over time, it got compressed into one word. Valorant's community wiki explicitly links it to "Trade," but veterans know the difference: a trade is the result, this is the action.

I personally think the best refrags come from players who expect their teammate to die. Dark, but true.

## FAQ

**Is this the same as trade?**

Close, but not identical. Trade is the 1-for-1 exchange. This is the specific kill that happens immediately after a teammate falls. All refrags are trades, but not all trades are refrags. Timing matters.

**When should I go for it?**

When your teammate dies in a tradeable position and you have the angle. If he's out in the open with three crossfires, don't feed. If he's door and you have the swing, go get it.

**How do I explain this to a non-gamer?**

It's like tag team wrestling. Your partner gets knocked down, and you immediately drop the guy who did it. The crowd goes wild. The round stays alive.

**Why do IGLs scream this so much?**

Because a missed follow-up loses rounds. A 4v5 is winnable. A 3v5 is basically over. The IGL knows the math, and the math says "get the damn kill."
