Back in 2006, QQ meant something completely different depending on who you asked. Old RTS players swore it was a rage-quit keyboard shortcut. Everyone else just saw two eyes leaking tears. Both camps were weirdly loud about it.

## A Quick History of QQ

Where did QQ come from? The simple version: those two letters look like a pair of eyes with tears running down. Type them at someone and you're calling them a crybaby. That's the whole insult.

The messier version involves Warcraft II. Legend says hitting Alt+Q+Q quit the match instantly, so typing it told a losing opponent to just leave already. The Warcraft Wiki treats that story as shaky at best, and the earliest written record of the term only shows up around 2006. WoW's trade chat is where it really went mainstream (looking at you, Barrens veterans), right next to timeless classics like "QQ more nub."

## More Than Just a Word

In WoW, it grew into a full vocabulary. "Less QQ more pew pew" was the standard forum answer to anyone complaining about class balance, usually right before the thread got locked.

Here's my honest observation after years in MMO and MOBA chats: the letters still live in WoW and other older communities. Players who grew up on Discord just drop the crying emoji and move on. Same insult, new packaging. Kind of wild that two capital letters outlasted entire expansions. If your lane opponent is already on [tilt](/slang/tilt-gaming/), a well-timed one is basically a finishing move. Why do gamers say QQ instead of just dropping the emoji? Old habits, older servers.

**New player trap:** it isn't an acronym. The Qs don't stand for anything. They're eyes. Crying eyes. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.

## Real Examples of QQ

Trade chat, 2009 energy:

> [2]BigMike: wtf why did they nerf paladins AGAIN
> [2]BigMike: this company hates me personally
> [2]XxSephirothxX: QQ more nub
> [2]BigMike: reported

All-chat, mid-ranked League game, as a support main I've received this exact energy:

Raid Discord, post-wipe, someone has opinions about the healing:

> "every pull we do the same thing and every pull we die to the same mechanic"
> "bro it's a one percent enrage, stop QQing and eat your feast"
> "im just saying"
> He was, in fact, just saying. For forty minutes.

## Common Questions

**QQ vs "crybaby": is it the same insult?**

Yes and no. The meaning is identical, but the delivery matters. Spelling out "crybaby" sounds like a scolding. Two little letters in chat is pure dismissal, lazy on purpose. That's why it tilts people so hard, and why it pairs so well with a dramatic [rage quit](/slang/rage-quit-gaming/).

**What do the two Qs actually stand for?**

Nothing, and if you came here asking "what does QQ mean in gaming," that's your answer. It's not initials. Each Q is an eye with a tear dropping off the bottom, so the pair reads as crying. People have invented backronyms for years, but the picture came first.

**Where will I run into it these days?**

Mostly older communities: WoW trade chat, MMO forums, the occasional League all-chat from a veteran. Younger players tend to type the crying emoji instead. When the letters do show up, they still land the same punch, especially next to someone going full [salty](/slang/salty-gaming/).
