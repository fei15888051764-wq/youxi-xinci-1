Every new player learns squishy the hard way. You facecheck one bush, the screen goes gray in under a second, and your support types "told you" before your body hits the floor.

## Where Squishy Came From

The squishy origin spent decades in toy aisles before it ever saw a health bar: squishy meant soft, squeezable, pleasant to crush. Competitive games adopted it in the 2000s because nothing else fit so well. StarCraft players had fragile spellcasters, MMOs had "clothies" in their cloth armor, and then League of Legends handed the label its forever home. The ADC who melts when an assassin glances at them? That one. No stat sheet uses the word, and it's one of those rare terms that needs zero translation between games.

## Why Everyone Says Squishy

Because teamfights need a target hierarchy and nobody has time to say "low effective health pool" out loud. The word sorts the room in two syllables: dive theirs, [peel](/slang/peel-gaming/) for yours, and maybe don't draft four of them into three assassins.

My one-line test after years of support duty: if one enemy rotation deletes you from full HP, you're the squishy. Fed or not. A 10/0 [glass cannon](/slang/glass-cannon-gaming/) still qualifies; it just shoots back on the way down.

### New Player Trap

Rookies hear "you're squishy" as slander and queue up more damage items. It's not slander, it's a care label. Buy the defensive item. Live through the burst. Deal your damage while alive, like a professional.

## Squishy in Real Conversations

Squishy examples from champ select to solo queue. Half insult, half care instruction.

Champ select, last pick hovers a carry into three assassins:

> **Me:** please no. we already have four squishies
> **LockInLarry:** i know their comp better than you
> **Me:** their comp is the reason
> **LockInLarry:** *locks in* trust

Solo queue, right after the river skirmish:

> **Jungle:** why did you walk up, you're the squishy
> **ADC:** i had flash
> **Jungle:** HAD

Your friend tries Dota for the first time:

> **NewGuy:** why does everyone call me squishy, my guy has armor
> **Me:** two items and a dream isn't armor
> **NewGuy:** rude
> **Me:** accurate. stand behind me

Tone check: it's affectionate, mostly. Somebody has to be told.

## Common Questions

**Squishy vs glass cannon: always the same thing?**

No, and mixing them up loses drafts. A glass cannon trades defense for huge damage on purpose. The word only comments on how fast you die. What does squishy mean when your tank says it? "Please stop walking first." Some carries are both. Some supports are neither.

**How do I stop being so breakable?**

Positioning first, items second. Stand behind the [tanky](/slang/tank-gaming/) friend, buy the defensive slot you keep skipping, and respect fog of war like it owes you money. Why do gamers say squishy so much in solo queue? Because somebody on every team refuses to do these three things.

**Where did the word even come from?**

Soft toys, believe it or not. The term jumped from plushies to RTS and MMO chatter in the 2000s, then MOBAs made it permanent. It stuck because it's perfect: one sound, and everyone pictures the same exploding ADC.
